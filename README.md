### Tools

- [**ksef-watch**](https://github.com/faceitall123qwe-hub/ksef-watch): Poland's e-invoicing system
  (KSeF) never tells you when someone invoices you. This polls it and sends the invoice, amount, due
  date and bank account to Telegram, with payment reminders and a VAT white list check.
- [**sitepitch**](https://github.com/faceitall123qwe-hub/sitepitch): finds local businesses with no
  website on OpenStreetMap and builds a demo site for each. Zero-dependency Python.
- [**telegram-bybit-copier**](https://github.com/faceitall123qwe-hub/telegram-bybit-copier): copies
  trade signals from Telegram to Bybit, with risk-based position sizing.
- [**telegram-notion-capture**](https://github.com/faceitall123qwe-hub/telegram-notion-capture):
  Telegram bot that turns voice notes and photos into Notion entries.
- [**taroluna**](https://github.com/faceitall123qwe-hub/taroluna): tarot app for iOS and Android.
  Expo, Supabase, RevenueCat.
